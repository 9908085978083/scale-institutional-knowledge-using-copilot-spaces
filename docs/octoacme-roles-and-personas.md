# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Facilitator

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master or Delivery Facilitator helps the team improve its delivery flow and working practices. This role facilitates team ceremonies and helps surface and resolve impediments; it does not replace the Project Manager's ownership of cross-team schedules, risks, and status reporting.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, daily coordination, reviews, and retrospectives as appropriate
- Help the team identify and remove delivery impediments
- Coach the team on iterative practices and continuous improvement
- Make workflow issues and blockers visible

### Interaction with Existing Roles
Partners with the Project Manager to escalate blockers and align team-level delivery with project plans. Works with Developers and QA to support effective ceremonies and continuous improvement, while the Project Manager remains accountable for cross-team coordination and reporting.

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
The Release Manager coordinates release readiness and the operational steps needed to deliver changes safely and communicate them clearly.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release plans, readiness checks, and deployment windows
- Confirm that approval, environment, verification, and rollback plans are clear
- Track release dependencies and readiness risks
- Coordinate release notes and timing communications

### Interaction with Existing Roles
Works with Developers and QA to assess implementation and test readiness, and with the Project Manager on schedules, dependencies, and stakeholder communications. Shares release status and timing with the Product Manager and stakeholders; deployment decisions follow the team's established ownership and approval process.

---

## Business Analyst / Requirements Lead

### Role Summary
The Business Analyst or Requirements Lead turns stakeholder needs into clear, validated requirements that the team can plan, build, and verify.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business and user needs
- Refine requirements, assumptions, and acceptance criteria
- Trace requirements to planned work and validation
- Identify ambiguity and coordinate resolution with relevant decision-makers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
Partners with the Product Manager to align requirements with product outcomes and priorities. Works with Developers and QA to make requirements testable and clarify acceptance criteria, and engages stakeholders to validate needs and resolve open questions. Product prioritization remains with the Product Manager.

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
The Security Lead helps the team identify and address security requirements and risks throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on security requirements and relevant threat scenarios
- Coordinate or conduct security design reviews and testing
- Track findings, remediation, and risk acceptance decisions
- Escalate critical security concerns through established channels

### Interaction with Existing Roles
Advises the Product Manager and Project Manager on security risks and their delivery implications, reviews designs with Developers, and coordinates with QA on appropriate security validation. Works with stakeholders and accountable decision-makers when findings require prioritization or risk acceptance.

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
The Data / Analytics Lead helps define how project and product outcomes will be measured and ensures that resulting data is useful and reliable.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success criteria into a measurement plan
- Coordinate instrumentation and data requirements
- Validate metrics, dashboards, and reporting assumptions
- Share outcome insights and measurement limitations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
Partners with the Product Manager to define and assess success metrics, and with Developers to plan instrumentation. Coordinates with QA to verify measurement behavior, shares findings with stakeholders, and brings relevant learning into Project Manager-led status reviews and retrospectives.

---

## Customer Support / Customer Success Liaison

### Role Summary
The Customer Support or Customer Success Liaison represents customer-facing support needs in planning, release preparation, and post-release learning.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer feedback, common support issues, and adoption needs
- Identify support impacts and prepare support teams for changes
- Help plan customer-facing guidance and escalation paths
- Communicate post-release issues and adoption signals to the team

### Interaction with Existing Roles
Works with the Product Manager to connect customer feedback to product decisions and with the Project Manager to coordinate readiness and communications. Partners with Developers and QA to surface real-world scenarios and post-release issues, and collaborates with the Release Manager and Technical Writer on support readiness and user guidance.

---

## Accessibility Lead

### Role Summary
The Accessibility Lead helps ensure that product requirements, design, implementation, and validation account for people with different access needs.

### Responsibilities
- Identify accessibility requirements and relevant standards
- Advise on inclusive design and implementation practices
- Coordinate accessibility reviews and testing coverage
- Track findings and work with the team on remediation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
Partners with the Product Manager to include accessibility in user and product requirements, advises Developers on implementation, and works with QA on accessibility test plans and verification. Coordinates with stakeholders and relevant design contributors to review needs and findings.

---

## Technical Writer / Documentation Owner

### Role Summary
The Technical Writer or Documentation Owner coordinates clear, accurate user-facing and operational documentation as part of delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify documentation needs and keep content accurate and usable
- Gather implementation details and review content with subject-matter experts
- Coordinate documentation review and readiness alongside product changes
- Maintain release notes, user guidance, or operational instructions as appropriate

### Interaction with Existing Roles
Works with the Product Manager to understand user needs and with Developers to confirm technical details. Coordinates review with QA and other subject-matter experts, and partners with the Project Manager and Release Manager to align documentation and communications with release timing.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Teams may combine or adapt roles to fit their size and context; when responsibilities overlap, make the accountable decision-maker and supporting roles explicit.
